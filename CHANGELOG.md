# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- Viewer count and activity feed, each with its own setting. The activity line shows the latest follow, sub, cheer, raid, or redemption, and how long ago it happened.
- Stream Preview and Quick Actions widgets, using the same Twitch login as chat. Preview is the popout player and starts silent. Quick Actions hides its panel header.
- Widgets can be edited, enabled, disabled, or deleted. They stay under the chat, are not always on top, and remember their position. Interactable widgets stay clickable when borders are hidden.
- Custom chat sounds can be imported.
- Settings, widgets, and custom sounds can be exported and imported.
- Auto-hide borders applies to the chat and to widgets.
- Updates are published from this repository.
